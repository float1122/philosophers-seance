# Product

## What
Philosophers' Séance is a web app styled as a candlelit Ouija board. The user summons the spirit of a dead public-domain philosopher and poses a personal worry; the spirit replies in character.

## Why
A costume-contest entry for Kiroween 2026: dress a chatbot as a séance. Pair the spooky séance aesthetic with genuine wisdom from public-domain thinkers, so the answer is both eerie and grounded rather than random spooky noise.

## Users
Casual visitors who want a playful, slightly spooky take on a personal worry, and philosophy-curious readers who enjoy hearing from Socrates, Confucius, Laozi, Seneca, Nietzsche, or Marcus Aurelius.

## Category
Kiroween 2026 hackathon — Costume Contest category.

## Out of scope
- Living or recently deceased thinkers whose work is still under copyright.
- Real divination, prophecy, or claims of contacting the dead.
- Medical, legal, financial, or therapeutic advice (see tone.md).
